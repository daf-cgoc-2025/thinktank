The Think Tank is the Department of the Air Force Company Grade Officers' Council (DAF CGOC) premier research opportunity. It fosters a collaborative environment to drive innovative solutions and proposals among the Company Grade Officer cohort.

Our mission is to increase research accessibility and opportunity for our young officers.

From cohort to Presentation Day.

Five steps, with Air University and the Think Tank Committee alongside the whole way.

  1. 01

    Match

    The final CGO cohort is matched, and Air University receives the list of participants.

  2. 02

    Kick-off

    Our Think Tank Committee leads hold a kick-off call with each cohort and set expectations and timelines.

  3. 03

    Research

    Research begins, with routine check-ins with an Air University research expert and the Think Tank Committee.

  4. 04

    Review

    The paper is completed and submitted to Air University and the Think Tank Committee for vetting and final review.

  5. 05

    Publish and present

    The paper is published, and Air University sets up a Presentation Day for the cohort to brief senior Air Force leadership.
