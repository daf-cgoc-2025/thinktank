From cohort to Presentation Day.

Five steps, with Air University and the Think Tank Committee alongside the whole way.

  1. 01

    Match

    The final CGO cohort is matched, and Air University receives the list of participants.

  2. 02

    Kick-off

    Our Think Tank Committee leads hold a kick-off call with each cohort and set expectations and timelines.

  3. 03

    Research

    Research begins, with routine check-ins with an Air University research expert and the Think Tank Committee.

  4. 04

    Review

    The paper is completed and submitted to Air University and the Think Tank Committee. Advisory panels pressure-test the research for analytical integrity and strategic readiness prior to Presentation Day.

  5. 05

    Publish and present

    The paper is published, and Air University sets up a Presentation Day for the cohort to brief senior Air Force leadership.

Who can apply
All Company Grade Officers employed by the Department of the Air Force are eligible to apply. That includes Space Force, Air National Guard, and Air Force Reserve officers.
Time commitment
Selected participants are expected to actively engage in all core program components. While the precise weekly hours may fluctuate depending on the phase of research and collaboration, acceptance into the program requires a steadfast commitment to dedicating sufficient time each week to contribute meaningfully, meet project milestones, and collaborate with peers.
